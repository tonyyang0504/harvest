# Live trials, 2026-10-01

Five new projects ran end to end on a Linux cloud host (datacenter IP), with real census, audit and build agents
(Claude Code, headless). The budget was `max_sources` 12, raised once to 18 with
`census-resume`. Builds covered the top 5 sources per project (two replacements for jobs, where three of the
five forbid collection). Runs used 2–3 pages per source. The proxy route and the browser user-agent mode stayed
off. Every defect found was fixed, each with a regression test.

| Project | Target, regions, record type | Sources found (collectable / blocked or none / deferred) | Allowed (lane, robots, terms) | Built (pass) | Enabled, rows stored / quarantined | Agent cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| swe-jobs | software engineering jobs, DE PL NL, `jobs` | 43 (16 / 27 / 12) | 8 | 7 builds: 4 pass, 3 stopped on `forbids` | 4 sources, 292 / 119 | $8.76 |
| tech-events | tech conferences and meetups, GB IE, `events` | 30 (16 / 14 / 10) | 11 | 5 (5) | 5 sources, 281 / 72 | $10.05 |
| solar-leads | solar panel installers, AE SA, `businesses` | 40 (7 / 33 / 0) | 4 | 5: 4 pass, 1 stopped on robots | 3 sources, 96 / 0 | $13.01 |
| used-phones | used smartphones, IN ID, `products` | 35 (17 / 18 / 0) | 11 | 5 (5) | 4 sources, 2,830 / 0 | $8.35 |
| office-rent | office space for rent, SG MY, `real_estate_rent` | 51 (14 / 37 / 0) | 11 | 5: 4 pass, 1 fail (no public prices) | 4 sources, 138 / 0 | $10.94 |

Agent spend was $51.11 in total: census and continuation $18.90, audits $13.67, builds $18.53. Exports are CSV
files, one per project; they are kept outside the repository.

## What each project showed

**swe-jobs.**
- Most of the market is closed to a datacenter client. 22 sites block it: 403 challenges at pracuj.pl,
  theprotocol.it, bulldogjob.pl, indeed and others; 403 IP blocks at olx.pl, adzuna.nl, youngcapital.nl and
  monsterboard.nl. stepstone.de and heise.de time out on robots.txt, which counts as a disallow.
- Five sites forbid automated collection in their terms, and the agents quoted the clauses:
  - justjoin.it §11.3: "Zakazane jest … automatyczne pobieranie";
  - germantechjobs.de §1.26 (a PDF);
  - nofluffjobs.com II.9 (a PDF that the operator read);
  - kleinanzeigen.de §5;
  - get-in-it.de.
- No Polish source survived.
- werkenvoornederland.nl publishes its content under CC0. Its salaries are monthly gross and convert ×12 to an
  annual figure. "Den Haag" and "'s-Gravenhage" both became The Hague.
- arbeitnow.com: 69 Swiss jobs are outside the project's regions. 50 more have a city in GB or FR while the module
  sets country DE (it reads the country from the site's domain). All 119 are quarantined.

**tech-events.**
- The robots fix made meetup.com (9 separate `User-agent: *` groups) correctly disallowed.
- eventslist.co.uk forbids scraping. Eventbrite answers 405, and 10times and IET answer with challenges.
- createwith.com and neventum.com list events worldwide. 71 rows from other countries are quarantined, plus 1 event that had already ended.
- After the date-level fingerprint fix, two groups dedup across sources (dev.events↔luma, createwith↔dev.events).
- luma.com's London feed is not topic-filtered (a book launch, a run club). This is a source-selection issue that
  the review cannot see.
- Times are local: "Tue, 14 Oct 2026, 18:30" becomes `+01:00` (BST).

**solar-leads.**
- The authoritative lists all block a datacenter client: DEWA's enrolled PV contractors (IP block), ADDC,
  Shamsi and Muqawil.
- Most open directories forbid collection: yello.ae, saudiayp.com, reachuae.com, buildeey.com, haraj.com.sa and
  2gis.ae.
- fronius.com's partner search is reachable only through endpoints that robots.txt disallows, so the builder
  stopped.
- What remains is yellowpages-uae.com (phones and P.O. boxes), listcompany.org, and RevenueBase's public top-25
  teaser. RevenueBase labels its own matches "indirect evidence", so they are weak leads.
- The census registered three one-off articles as sources. The brief now forbids that. One article,
  projectsegy.com, had its "terms" taken from another article's URL; its real terms prohibit copying.

**used-phones.**
- The open market is refurbishers on Shopify or WooCommerce: budli.in, refitglobal.com and isecondphone.com
  (Indonesia). OLX IN and OLX ID time out. Quikr and Blibli answer with IP blocks. Tokopedia and Bukalapak
  disallow the search path in robots.txt. kitar.id, maujual.com and grest.in forbid collection.
- refitglobal stores one row per Shopify variant, and 2,289 of its 2,448 variants are out of stock.
- Used phones carry no GTIN, so cross-source dedup cannot match them.
- The FX conversions checked out (13,499 INR → 140.6 USD at 96.0).
- lazada.co.id passed review but was disabled: its terms link is a JavaScript router URL and the help-centre
  terms do not render, so the verdict stays `unknown`.

**office-rent.**
- 37 of 51 sources are lane `none`:
  - every major portal answers with a challenge (PropertyGuru SG/MY, 99.co, iProperty, EdgeProp MY, SRX);
  - government and broker sites (HDB, SLA, JLL, Savills, Cushman & Wakefield) answer with an IP block;
  - edgeprop.sg, propnex.com and ibilik.com forbid collection.
- Collected: jtc.gov.sg (business-park units with rent and area), punb.com.my (64 shop-offices with RM rent and
  m²), workin.space (SG+MY) and commonground.work.
- Coworking prices are per seat ("From RM799 / Seat / Month"). The new `price_basis` field marks this when the
  price text is passed through, and the build brief now requires it.

**Census evidence.** Every non-blocked source's evidence URL was fetched again through harvest's client. Quoted
titles, IDs and counts were found on the live pages (for example "Developer jobs in Netherlands | 13,429 open
jobs", "Kedai Pejabat Teres 4 Tingkat", "SGD 748–SGD 785"). The claimed 403s and challenges reproduced. No
fabricated source or citation was found. The only misses were time-varying lists, such as Shopify's newest
products.

## Defects found and fixed

| Area | Defect (how it showed) | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| robots.txt | Only the first `User-agent: *` group was used. meetup.com and flipkart.com repeat it 9 and 11 times, so their search paths looked allowed. Substring agent matching also applied other crawlers' groups. | RFC 9309: every group for the exact product token (else every `*` group) is merged |
| Terms | A PDF terms file was read as bytes and classified `no_clause`, which the enable gate accepts. This hit nofluffjobs, germantechjobs and jobware.de live. | A binary or PDF document is `unknown` |
| Terms | "Do not scrape…" was not seen as a prohibition. Dutch, Indonesian/Malay and Arabic were missing, and so was Polish "zakazane". | Patterns added |
| Terms | The terms link was an article whose slug contains "conditions" (projectsegy), and hrefs were not unescaped (`&amp;`, lazada). | Links labelled as terms win; short slugs only; unescape |
| HTTP | An Imperva/Incapsula interstitial served with HTTP 200 was treated as content, and detection called it a JS shell (browser lane). | A 200 response that has a bot-wall script and no text is a challenge |
| Lanes | A JSON API URL was labelled `html`; a feed URL was labelled `sitemap`; their terms were never found. | Source-URL documents are read first; terms come from the home page |
| Lanes | A site-wide WordPress `/feed/` won the lane for 9 sources. | A feed must carry the page's inventory |
| Lanes | For `businesses`, a site's own Organization JSON-LD counted as listings (3 articles got `embedded_json`). | Only entities that are not the site describing itself count |
| Lanes | Six builders built on key-less JSON endpoints and had to call them `openapi`. | New `json_api` lane, with a scaffold template and brief text |
| Census | `raw.githubusercontent.com`, Substack and Buttondown sources were keyed by the shared host. | Keyed by owner (path or subdomain) |
| Census | Plan queries for jobs asked for "dealers … inventory"; three angles had identical queries. | Queries per record type |
| Census | `census-resume --agent` started an agent even when the deferred candidates refilled the budget (jobs and events: "nothing new could enter"). Under inline mode the CLI killed it. | Skipped with a reason; the CLI waits |
| Census | Articles, listicles, PDFs and API docs were registered as sources; a dead domain was recorded as "blocked". | Brief rules (census and audit) |
| Normalise | A multi-region source used its first region for every row (Dublin events became GB). | The country comes from the row (field, else gazetteer city) |
| Normalise | Out-of-region rows were stored, and a row whose city lies in another country was accepted. | Quarantined with the reason |
| Normalise | Events that had ended (back to 2009) were stored as listings. | Template `expires` |
| Normalise | Event fingerprints used the full timestamp, so cross-source dedup never matched. | Compared by date |
| Normalise | Business dedup compared raw website and name strings; offices never got a fingerprint (the key needed bedrooms). | URLs by host and path, names without legal suffixes, a key without bedrooms |
| Normalise | `RM799` gave no amount and `Rp5.000.000` gave 0. `sq ft` was read as HUF. Arabic-Indic digits, juta/ribu, riyal/dirham and Dhs were not parsed. | Fixed |
| Normalise | Dutch, Polish, Indonesian/Malay and Arabic period words, and `zł/h`, were missing. A default period was set on rows with no salary. | Fixed |
| Normalise | Month-name dates ("14 Oct 2026", "15 października 2026") failed. Times were dropped, and naive times were stored as UTC. | Nine languages, times, local zones, year inference |
| Normalise | Per-seat and psf prices were stored as unit rents. | `price_basis` |
| Gazetteer | NL, IE, SG, MY and ID were missing; "Utrecht (NDW)", "London EC2A 4NE" and "Berlin; Munich" were not matched. | Cities added; qualifier, postcode and list variants |

## Proxy and user-agent decisions

Both modes stayed off for every run. One source qualified for the pre-approved proxy exception:
siliconrepublic.com (tech-events). It answers with a plain 403 IP block, with no challenge, and it publishes no
terms of use; the footer links only a privacy policy, read from a non-datacenter IP. Its verdict is recorded as
`no_clause`, and `harvest proxy enable tech-events --source siliconrepublic_com --reason "trial 2026-10 IP block"`
is stored in its `lane_detail.proxy`.

The trial host had no proxy pool configured for harvest (`pool_configured: false`), so the route stayed inert and
the source was not collected.

The other IP-blocked sources did not qualify, because their terms could not be read or were not checked:
adzuna.nl, monsterboard.nl, youngcapital.nl, olx.pl, DEWA, HDB (a JavaScript terms page), SLA, JLL, quikr,
blibli and opensooq.

## Remaining limits

- The trials found no fabricated census evidence. Even so, agents still sometimes pick sources off target: Luma
  is not topic-filtered, RevenueBase's teaser offers "indirect evidence", and listcompany.org mixes battery and UPS
  traders. The review checks the schema, not relevance.
- A PDF terms document needs a person or an agent with a PDF reader. harvest now says `unknown` instead of
  guessing.
- The blog-feed rule is a heuristic. voltiat.com and worq.space still link their posts from the listing page, so
  they keep lane `feed`. The builder chooses the real surface.
- Datacenter egress closes most market leaders in all five verticals. The open remainder is refurbishers,
  government landlords, small directories and aggregators.
